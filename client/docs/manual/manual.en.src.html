1 Overview and terms

The Add-on Store brings extensions for Tungsten Power PDF right into the program. You find add-ons in a catalog, read their description, screenshots and ratings, and install, update or remove them with one click. The store also keeps itself and your add-ons up to date and tells you when an update for Power PDF is available.

This manual describes the client: the add-on in Power PDF with the store window. The store website and the portal for developers and administrators of the store server are described in the overall Add-on Store manual.

What the Add-on Store does

  • Shows the catalog of add-ons in the language of Power PDF: name, description, category, version, size, author, screenshots, ratings and "What’s new in" the current version.
  • Installs, updates and removes add-ons; every change needs one administrator approval.
  • Installs only reviewed and signed packages: the store's signature and the checksum (SHA-256) are checked before anything is copied.
  • Announces new versions: a dot on the ribbon button, the view Available updates and, if you like, a one-time notice.
  • Updates itself: Power PDF closes, the new setup runs and Power PDF starts again.
  • Searches by need: you describe what you need, the store suggests matching add-ons.
  • Unlocks add-ons for your organization with a customer code from your add-on provider.
  • Sends problems and suggestions to the developer of an add-on, with attachments and, if you like, the log.
  • Lets you rate add-ons with stars.
  • Detects blocked add-ons and offers to remove them.
  • Switches add-ons that the store takes out of service for now off and on again, without removing them.

Help

The Add-on Store brings one help for all installed add-ons, in which you search all help texts at once. You open it like this:

  • on the Enhanced Features tab with the Help button (on the far right, group Help);
  • in the store window with Help at the top right or with F1: the help goes straight to the chapter about this window;
  • in every add-on with its Help button or F1: the help goes to the matching chapter of the add-on;
  • in the classic window with Help, on the options page with Power PDF's help.

On the left are the installed add-ons; a click opens or closes their chapters. On the right is the chosen chapter, with Previous and Next at the bottom and Print chapter at the top. Type one or more words into Search the help at the top: the results appear at once, with add-on, chapter and an excerpt; upper and lower case and accents do not matter, and every word must occur. Up and down arrow choose a result, Enter opens it with the search words highlighted, Esc clears the search. Links to websites open in the web browser. F1 in the help window shows this section.

The help appears in the language of Power PDF when an add-on brings it, otherwise in English. The help of the Add-on Store is available in English, French, Italian, German, Spanish and Dutch.

2 Requirements and installation

Requirements

  • Tungsten Power PDF Business SaaS (with sign-in), version 2025.3.7 or later. In Power PDF 2025 only the Business edition loads add-ons; from 2026.4 on every edition does (Standard, Advanced, Business).
  • The setup refuses a Power PDF licensed with a serial number; the Add-on Store is meant for Power PDF with sign-in only.
  • Windows 10 or 11, x64 or ARM64.
  • For the modern store window the Microsoft WebView2 runtime (usually present in Windows 10 and 11). Without it the classic window opens.
  • An HTTPS connection (port 443) to the store server, direct or through the system proxy.
  • To install, update and remove add-ons: local administrator rights (one prompt per action).

Installation

  1. On the Add-on Store website, choose Download the Add-on Store. You get the setup PluginStore.msi.
  2. Close Power PDF and start the setup. On the first download Windows SmartScreen may warn; then choose More info and Run anyway.
  3. Read the license, tick I accept the license and choose Install. Windows asks once for administrator rights.
  4. Start Power PDF. The ribbon shows the Store tab with the Add-on Store button.

The setup finds Power PDF by itself (through HKLM\SOFTWARE\Kofax\PDF\V1, else through App Paths) and installs into its bin\Plug-Ins folder. When Power PDF is still running, the setup offers to close it and start it again afterwards. The setup comes in all 21 languages of Power PDF; Windows picks the user's language.

Removing it

Remove Add-on Store for Tungsten Power PDF under Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps. The setup removes only the store's own files; add-ons you installed stay in Power PDF. Remove them in the store window first if you no longer need them.

3 Quick start

  1. On the Store tab, click Add-on Store. The store window opens.
  2. Look through the list of add-ons on the left, or type into Search add-ons at the top.
  3. Click an add-on. Its description, screenshots and ratings appear on the right.
  4. Choose Install and confirm. Windows asks once for administrator rights.
  5. When asked about the restart, choose Restart now. Power PDF loads add-ons only at start; unsaved documents are offered for saving first.

After the restart the add-on is on the Enhanced Features tab (or where its description says).

5 The store window

The window opens at a fitting size (about 1000 × 680 points, never larger than the screen) and shows the last catalog it loaded right away; it fetches the current one in the background. Until the first catalog is there, a loading indicator turns.

The header

  • Search add-ons: the search box (chapter 6).
  • Customer code: manages the customer codes (chapter 10). A tick shows that a code is active.
  • All add-ons: only in the view Available updates; back to the full list.
  • Help: opens the help at the chapter about this window (also with F1).

Banners

Below the header, coloured bars across the window appear when needed: a new version of the Add-on Store, an update for Power PDF, blocked add-ons, add-ons from an earlier Power PDF installation, a lock by IT, or a connection error. Chapters 8 and 11 explain them.

Categories and author

Below the banners are category buttons: All and one per category. With an active customer code, the For button with the name of your organization comes first; it shows only the add-ons delivered to you. When there are add-ons from at least two authors, choose one under Author.

The list

On the left the add-ons appear as rows with icon, name, short description and stars. At the top is the section Installed with the number of installed add-ons and, if any, Updates with their number; below it More add-ons. Among the installed ones, those delivered with a customer code come first, then those you installed yourself, then those no longer offered; in each group updates first, then by name.

On the right of each row is the matching action: Install, Remove, or for an update the split button Update; its arrow opens a menu with Update and Remove.

On the right, for the chosen add-on: icon, name, category, Version, size, the installed version when an update waits, stars, the description, screenshots (a click enlarges them), What’s new in the version, Connects to (chapter 11), Author with contact address (a click starts an email), the actions, Report a problem and Your rating. Below is the note that installing and updating need administrator rights.

The footer

At the bottom are the short disclaimer, the link Disclaimer with the full text, and Report a problem with the Add-on Store.

Customer codes, ratings, problem reports, the search by need and the updates view are not available there.

6 Searching and filtering

While typing

What you type into Search add-ons filters the list at once by name, description and author. Upper and lower case do not matter. Category and author apply on top. When nothing matches, the list says "No add-ons found."

Search by need

Describe in your own words what you need, for example "split invoices by barcode", and press Enter (at least two characters). The store server looks for matching add-ons and ranks them; when the store's AI assistant is on, each hit carries a short reason. The bar shows "Best matches for: …" or "No add-on found for: …". Show all, Esc or typing on ends the search by need.

7 Install, update, remove

Installing

  1. Choose Install. Confirm the question "Install '…' version … now?" with Install.
  2. The client downloads the package, checks checksum and signature and then asks once for administrator rights. Meanwhile "Installing…" is shown.
  3. After copying it asks whether Power PDF should restart. Restart now closes Power PDF (unsaved documents are offered for saving) and starts it again; Later loads the add-on at the next start.

An add-on that needs a newer version of Power PDF shows "Requires Power PDF … or later" instead of Install.

Updating

For an update choose Update, in the list, in the details or in Available updates. It works like installing. The store offers only newer versions; an older version never replaces an installed one.

Removing

Choose Remove and confirm "Remove '…' from Power PDF now?". After the administrator approval the client deletes the add-on's file, its data folder and its user settings. Power PDF unloads the add-on at the next start. The store can only remove add-ons it installed itself (recognised by the file manifest.json in the add-on's data folder); it also recognises add-ons from a manual installation (chapter 13) that way.

What protects you

  • Only what a trusted store signed is installed, and the SHA-256 checksum must match. Both are checked once more before copying, in a folder only administrators may write to.
  • The client talks only to the configured store server over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or later) and follows no redirect.
  • The step with administrator rights runs only when you install, update or remove; everything else runs with your normal rights.

8 Updates

How the store finds updates

About 15 seconds after Power PDF starts, and shortly after the store window closes, the client reads the catalog in the background. When it finds a newer version of the Add-on Store or of an installed add-on, the Add-on Store and Updates buttons get an amber dot. When the hint about Power PDF updates is on, an update for Power PDF counts too.

The view "Available updates"

The Updates button on the Help tab opens the store window in this view. It shows one card for Power PDF, for the Add-on Store and for each installed add-on with an update, each with "Installed: … · New: …" and the "What's new" text.

  • Update or Remove per add-on; with more than one update Update all (one after the other, one restart question at the end).
  • Update Add-on Store on the store's card (section below).
  • What's new and Hide on the Power PDF card.
  • All add-ons at the top right leads to the full list. With nothing to update it says "Everything is up to date."

One-time notice

With the option Notify me once about each new update on (the default), Power PDF asks once for a new update: "Updates are available: … Open the Add-on Store now?". Yes opens the view Available updates. The notice does not come again for the same update. While a dialog is open, the notice waits until it is closed.

Updating the Add-on Store itself

When there is a new version of the store, the bar "A new version of the Add-on Store is available: … (installed: …)" appears at the top with Update Add-on Store. The client downloads the package, checks checksum and signature and asks: "Power PDF closes for the update, …". After Yes Power PDF closes, the new setup runs with a progress bar (one administrator prompt) in the language of Power PDF, and Power PDF starts again on its own, also when the setup was cancelled.

Hint about updates for Power PDF

When the store administrators offer an update for your Power PDF version, the top shows "An update for Power PDF is available: …", with What's new (opens Tungsten Automation's official release notes in the browser) and Hide (until the next newer version). The store cannot install Power PDF: "Your IT or a person with administrator rights installs the update." The option for this is on the options page.

9 What's new, ratings, problem reports

What's new

After an update, by the store, by IT or by the self-update, the store window shows What's new once the next time it opens: the text of the new version for each add-on and for the store. Got it closes the overview. What you installed in the store window yourself counts as seen already.

Ratings

Each add-on shows its average and the number of ratings. You rate installed add-ons under Your rating with one to five stars; another click changes the rating. One rating counts per installation. For add-ons that are not installed it says "Install the add-on to rate it."

Report a problem

  1. In the details choose Report a problem (for the store itself, Report a problem with the Add-on Store at the bottom).
  2. Choose Problem or Comment or suggestion and describe what happened (at least five characters, at most 4000).
  3. If you like, enter an Email for questions, tick Attach the log of the last store actions, and add up to 3 files with Attach files (PNG, JPEG, PDF, TXT, LOG; at most 5 MB each, 10 MB together).
  4. Choose Send.

The report goes to the developer of the add-on. The versions of the add-on, Power PDF and Windows are sent along; no documents. Do not attach confidential documents.

10 Customer codes

A customer code unlocks add-ons your add-on provider delivers to your organization. It looks like this: XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

  1. In the header choose Customer code.
  2. Enter the code and choose Add code. The store checks it and says, for example, "Code valid for …: 3 add-on(s) unlocked."
  3. Choose Close. The unlocked add-ons appear in the list with the label "For …" and under the category button For.

Customer codes on this computer lists every code (shortened) with organization, unlocked add-ons and, when the delivery is limited, the seats in use, for example "Installations: 3/10". Up to 10 codes are possible.

Remove code asks "Remove the customer code of …?". Add-ons that came with this code only are removed from Power PDF as well (one administrator confirmation); when that is declined, the code stays. When IT sets the codes by policy, the dialog says "Your IT manages the customer code on this computer." and nothing can be changed.

13 Links from the website, manual installation

"Install in Power PDF"

On the store website every add-on has the button Install in Power PDF. Through the link addonstore:// it opens the store window with this add-on and asks whether to install it; when Power PDF is still starting, the client waits until its main window is up. A website can only open the window this way, never install anything by itself. When the add-on is not in the catalog, the window says "The add-on “…” from the website is not in the catalog. It may only be in the beta channel (Options)."

Manual installation without the client

For computers without the client the website offers Manual (ZIP) for each public add-on: a ZIP with install.bat, uninstall.bat and instructions. install.bat checks signature and checksum, asks once for administrator rights and sets up the ribbon group; /quiet installs without questions for software distribution. A manual installation gets no updates and no security blocks. When the client is installed later, it recognises the add-on and keeps it up to date. When the client removes such an add-on, it also deletes its entry under Installed apps; when it updates it, it updates the version shown there.

14 For administrators

Deployment

The MSI installs per machine and runs silently, best as its own step right after Power PDF (Intune, SCCM, GPO, scripts):

msiexec /i PluginStore.msi /qn /norestart /l*v "%TEMP%\AddonStore.log"

  • Power PDF must be installed first. Whether it was ever started does not matter.
  • The setup refuses when Power PDF is missing, older than 2025.3.7 (checked with VersionLong in HKLM\SOFTWARE\Kofax\PDF\V1), an edition without add-ons before 2026.4, or licensed with a serial number. For the last case the property ALLOWNONSAAS=1 installs anyway.
  • Force a language with TRANSFORMS=:<LCID>, for example TRANSFORMS=:1031 for German.
  • Intune detection rule: the file <bin>\Plug-Ins\PluginStore.zxt with a minimum version (the ProductCode changes with every version).
  • A fresh installation (not an update) clears leftovers of removed add-ons from the Enhanced Features tab once per user; installed add-ons add their groups again at their next start.
  • When IT deploys the MSI itself, DisableSelfUpdate switches the self-update off.

Policies

All policies live under HKLM\SOFTWARE\Kofax\PDF\Tungsten Power PDF\PluginStore\Policies\Store (64-bit view; from 32-bit deployment agents use reg add … /reg:64). They beat the user's settings and lock the matching fields of the options page. DWORD values may also be set as text such as "1" (REG_SZ). The client reads them when Power PDF starts.

Logs

  • %TEMP%\PluginStore.log: log of the client and its helper steps (restart, self-update, switch to the second address). More details with Verbose logging.
  • %WINDIR%\Temp\AddonStoreUpdate-<id>.log: the MSI log of a self-update.

Network

  • One firewall rule is enough: HTTPS (port 443) to the store server, through the system proxy (WinHTTP automatic proxy detection). TLS 1.2 or later, no redirects, no other host, even if the catalog named one.
  • With the built-in address the rule needs two names: addon.power-pdf.de and ppdf-store.azurewebsites.net.
  • Second address: when a request to https://addon.power-pdf.de fails (no connection, TLS or certificate error, dropped answer, HTTP error), the client sends it once to https://ppdf-store.azurewebsites.net, but only after that host identified itself as the Add-on Store (GET /api/ping). It then stays there for the running session and asks the main address again every 10 minutes. A POST that may have reached the server is never sent twice. This applies only with the built-in address, not with ServerUrl or another address in the options.
  • Background checks: license approval 3 seconds after start and every 4 hours, block list after 20 seconds and every 4 hours, updates after about 15 seconds and after the store window closes.

Privacy

The client sends no documents and no names of users or computers. Every request carries technical data to the store server:

  • the program identifier with the versions of client, Power PDF and Windows and the processor architecture, for example AddonStore-PowerPDF/{VERSION} (PowerPDF …; Windows …; x64);
  • the license mode of Power PDF (cloud, serial or unknown), so the store can check whether it may be used;
  • only with a customer code: the codes and a random installation id of this user; the server stores only a check value of it per delivery.

Ratings use the same random id; the server stores only a check value of it per add-on. Reports contain what you enter and attach, plus the versions of the add-on, Power PDF and Windows.

Once a day the client sends an inventory report: the random installation id, the domain of the Power PDF sign-in (only the part after the @, never the address; domains of public mail services are not sent), the license mode, the versions of Power PDF and the client, and the installed add-ons with their versions. The server evaluates it only when its administrators switched this evaluation on. The policy Inventory = 0 switches the report off.

Clarify the use with your data protection officer; the statement of the operator of the store server applies to the data on the server.

Security

  • Only packages signed by a trusted store key are installed (built in: the store's key and a recovery key; further keys only by the policy TrustedSigningKeys, never through HKCU).
  • The step with administrator rights starts Windows PowerShell by its full path with the script in memory. It first copies the package into a folder under Plug-Ins that only administrators may write to, checks the checksum there again and only then unpacks it.
  • The store window shows a page built into the client. It loads nothing from the internet, cannot navigate and talks to the client only through messages; data appears as text only.
  • The link helper AddonStoreLink.exe passes only a checked add-on id; nothing is installed before you confirm.

17 Frequently asked questions

Are the add-ons official Tungsten Automation products?

No. They come from independent developers and are provided without warranty and without official support; see Disclaimer. The author named in the catalog answers questions.

Why do I need administrator rights?

Add-ons live in Power PDF's program folder under Program Files, which only administrators may write to. That is why the store asks once per action.

Why does Power PDF have to restart?

Power PDF loads add-ons only at start and releases them only when it ends.

Does the store see my documents?

No. The client sends no documents; chapter 14 lists everything it sends.

Can I go back to an older version of an add-on?

The store installs no older versions. Remove the add-on and ask its author.

Does the store work without internet?

Installed add-ons keep working. The window shows the last catalog it loaded; installing and updating work again once there is a connection.

How do I get add-ons made for my company?

With your provider's customer code (chapter 10), or IT sets it by policy.
